## 1. Entry-point map — 10 lines (OC2B-276)

1. `os/worlds/science-lab/hortus/engine.py::run_evolution` — the GA loop (tournament select, crossover, mutate, elite carry); caller sets seed/pop/gens/env.
2. `os/worlds/science-lab/hortus/model.py::score` — fitness: toy-fin heat watts minus spacing-margin and mass-cap penalties; deterministic.
3. `os/worlds/science-lab/hortus/model.py::heat_watts` — raw Q from the analytic fin model (no penalties).
4. `os/worlds/science-lab/hortus/model.py::geometry` — feasibility facts (spacing, mass, spacing_ok, mass_ok).
5. `os/worlds/science-lab/hortus/verifier.py::verify_champion` — held-out gate: feasible + beats reference by 1.05x in ALL three held-out envs.
6. `os/worlds/science-lab/hortus/records.py::RecordWriter` — append-only JSONL run log (run_start/birth/death/generation/verdict/run_end).
7. `os/worlds/science-lab/hortus/records.py::reconstruct` — rebuilds the family tree from the log; raises on gaps.
8. `os/worlds/science-lab/hortus/run_demo.py::main` — shipped 300-gen demo entry point (not used here; this run calls the engine directly).
9. `os/worlds/science-lab/breeding.py::propose_variants/select_survivor` — experiment-plan variant layer (NOT heatsink breeding; recorded so the two are not confused).
10. `labs/hortus/runs/run-001/run.py` — THIS run's driver: fixed params below, writes generations.csv, winner.json, fitness.svg, records.jsonl.

Selection (OC2B-290): run-001 selected for maximum fin area past the mass
limit — 34 fins at maxed-out 60.0 mm height with thin 0.93 mm fins on a
4.61 mm base, 0.510 kg over the 0.45 kg cap, so the champion is
mass-infeasible even on its training turf and the held-out gate rejected
it (`rejected`: feasible=false in all three held-out envs, though it beats
the reference on raw heat everywhere). The fitness penalty (200 W/kg over)
was outrun by raw area gain — an honest overfitting-to-training-penalty
finding.
