## Brief

- Sound: head-nod groove music. Deep sine bass on the beat, a
  laid-back triangle melody, dry noise hats on the offbeats.
  Warm and steady; the groove sits, it never rushes.
- Structure: three bars — a bass-led intro bar, a melody bar
  over the same groove, and a closing bar landing back on the
  tonic.
- Parameters: tempo 90, key A, mode minor, waveform triangle,
  bars 3.
- Constraints: bass deep and forward; melody laid-back, never
  flashy; hats dry and steady on the offbeats. Straight
  offbeats in v1 — swing is a future brief parameter, not
  rendered yet.

## Keeper record (measured, not claimed)

- 4 takes at these exact params, 4 keepers (session
  hn038-boom-bap). Small sample: do not quote 100% as a rate —
  four takes prove the brief renders and passes, nothing more.

## How to use it

- Use for "groove", "head-nod", or "hip-hop beat" asks.
- Do NOT claim swing: the loop plays straight offbeats. If
  someone asks for swing, say it is a future brief parameter
  and offer straight boom-bap instead — never present a
  straight take as swung.
