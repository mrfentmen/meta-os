## Brief

- Sound: slow pad music. Soft triangle pads, a sparse melody
  that leaves space between notes, quiet hats far behind the
  mix. Gentle from the first bar; nothing sharp, nothing
  hurried.
- Structure: two long bars — a pad bed stating the progression,
  then a second bar where the sparse melody enters over the
  same bed.
- Parameters: tempo 72, key F, mode major, waveform triangle,
  bars 2.
- Constraints: attacks soft and levels low; melody sparse, with
  rests; hats quiet; no driving rhythm, no bright leads.

## Keeper record (measured, not claimed)

- 4 takes at these exact params, 4 keepers (session
  hn038-ambient). Small sample: do not quote 100% as a rate —
  four takes prove the brief renders and passes, nothing more.
- Major mode gets the quieter hat gain (0.06) in the loop.

## How to use it

- Use for "slow", "calm", "gentle", or "background" asks.
- If someone wants ambient longer than two bars, extend the
  bars in a NEW brief file and measure it — do not stretch a
  take after rendering and call it the same brief.
