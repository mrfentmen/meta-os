## Brief

- Sound: fast square-wave chip music. Bright leads, arpeggiated
  chords, driving bass, crisp noise hats on the offbeats.
  Energy high from the first bar; nothing drifts or fades out.
- Structure: four bars — a chord intro stating the progression,
  a lead bar walking the scale, a variation bar, and a closing
  bar landing back on the tonic.
- Parameters: tempo 132, key C, mode minor, waveform square,
  bars 4.
- Constraints: lead bright and forward; pads stay behind the
  lead; hats on every offbeat; no slow swells, no sparse
  passages.

## Keeper record (measured, not claimed)

- 116 takes at these exact params, 100 keepers (86%).
- Sessions: hn029-chipsynth 12 takes / 11 keepers,
  hn038-chipsynth 4 / 4, overnight-001 100 / 85.
- All 16 rejects at these params were repetition rejects
  (11 at exactly 0.750, 5 at 1.000). None failed on length,
  loudness, or silence.

## How to use it

- This is the workhorse brief: the biggest keeper sample in the
  pack, and the default when someone asks for "chip" or "fast"
  music without more detail.
- If a chipsynth session's keeper rate drops below the 86%
  line, the first lever is melody variation (wider steps, more
  rests), not tempo or key — the rejects say repetition, not
  sound.
