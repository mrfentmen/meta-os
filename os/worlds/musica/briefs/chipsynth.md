# Chipsynth brief

## Sound

Fast square-wave chip music: bright leads, arpeggiated chords,
driving bass, and crisp noise hats on the offbeats. Energy high
from the first bar; nothing drifts or fades out.

## Structure

Four bars: a chord intro that states the progression, a lead bar
that walks the scale, a variation bar, and a closing bar that
lands back on the tonic.

## Parameters

tempo: 132
key: C
mode: minor
waveform: square
bars: 4

## Constraints

Keep the lead bright and forward. Pads stay behind the lead.
Hats on every offbeat. No slow swells, no sparse passages.
