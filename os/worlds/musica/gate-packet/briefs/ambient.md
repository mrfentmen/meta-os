# Ambient brief

## Sound

Slow pad music: soft triangle pads, a sparse melody that leaves
space between notes, and quiet hats far behind the mix. Gentle
from the first bar; nothing sharp, nothing hurried.

## Structure

Two long bars: a pad bed that states the progression, then a
second bar where the sparse melody enters over the same bed.

## Parameters

tempo: 72
key: F
mode: major
waveform: triangle
bars: 2

## Constraints

Keep attacks soft and levels low. Melody sparse, with rests.
Hats quiet. No driving rhythm, no bright leads.
