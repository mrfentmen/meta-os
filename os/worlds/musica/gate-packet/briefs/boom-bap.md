# Boom-bap brief

## Sound

Head-nod groove music: deep sine bass on the beat, a laid-back
triangle melody, and dry noise hats on the offbeats. Warm and
steady; the groove sits, it never rushes.

## Structure

Three bars: a bass-led intro bar, a melody bar over the same
groove, and a closing bar that lands back on the tonic.

## Parameters

tempo: 90
key: A
mode: minor
waveform: triangle
bars: 3

## Constraints

Bass deep and forward. Melody laid-back, never flashy. Hats dry
and steady on the offbeats. Straight offbeats in v1 (swing is a
future brief parameter, not rendered yet).
