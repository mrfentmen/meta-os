# Meta Mother Tongue — demo still

Companion to the Meta Mother Tongue spec (branch `milo/mother-tongue-spec`).
Applications only. No OS work, no merge — branch only, for Pax/Del's check-in.

## What this is

One demo still for tonight's demo: `docs/mother-tongue-demo-still.webp`.

It shows the idea in one picture: a fictional elderly storyteller telling a
story to a child, with a small audio recorder on the table between them.
The app's job is the recording, the transcription, and the lessons that let
kids learn their own language back — with the speaker's permission, and with
the community in control of the recordings.

## Fictional, on purpose

The people in the still are a fictional illustration. They are not a real
speaker, a real language, or a real community. No real elder is pictured,
named, or impersonated. A demo video with a real speaker only happens with
that speaker's own permission — that piece is still owed, not faked here.

## Demo flow the still stands for

1. An elder agrees to record a story or everyday speech, and says how it
   may be used.
2. The app records, transcribes, and keeps the recording under the
   community's control.
3. Kids get short lessons built from those recordings, in their own
   language.
